WA-agent

A private, internal tool. It is not offered to the public.

What it does

WA-agent keeps a task tracker for one business. It reads the WhatsApp client groups the business has allowed, and the Google Meet recordings of the business's own meetings. It turns them into a spreadsheet of open tasks and meeting summaries in the business's own Google Drive.

Who uses it

Only the business that runs it, on its own computer. It is not sold, and no one outside the business can sign up.
